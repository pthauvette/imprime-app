# 🖨️ Order from Plio inside Claude

Connect Plio to your AI assistant to browse, get pricing, and place your print
orders right inside the conversation.

> French version: [mcp-connecteur-client.md](mcp-connecteur-client.md)

## Add it (one time)

1. In Claude: **Settings → Connectors → Add connector**.
2. Paste this address:

   ```
   https://www.plio.ca/api/mcp/mcp
   ```

3. Click **Connect** and sign in with **the email address of your Plio account**.

✅ That's it. *No Plio account yet? Connecting creates one automatically — just use
the email you want to keep.*

> ⚠️ **Important — one email only.** Use **the same address** in the AI and on
> plio.ca. That's what links your account: you'll be able to **track your orders and
> reorder** from Claude as well as on the website.

## What you can ask

- *"What's the price for 500 business cards, 14 pt, double-sided?"*
- *"Estimate shipping to Montréal for 1,000 flyers."*
- *"Place an order for 250 postcards"* → you get a **secure link** to upload your
  file and pay on plio.ca.

Prices and shipping are **always identical** to the website.
